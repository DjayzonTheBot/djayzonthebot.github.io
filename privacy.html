Djayzon - Privacy Policy

Djayzon only connects to the owner's own TikTok account in order to upload videos to it. The TikTok access token is stored privately on the owner's server and is used only to publish videos.

The app does not collect, sell or share any personal data from other users, and it does not read any other user's content. The videos are gameplay recordings from a private Minecraft server.

To request deletion of any stored data, contact: extraspaceinformation@gmail.com
